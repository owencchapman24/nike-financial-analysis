# Scenario rationale

## Interpretation

Base, bull, and bear are documented project analyst scenarios, not Nike guidance,
consensus estimates, probabilities, or investment recommendations. They use only
information available through September 7, 2026. Exact full-precision values,
historical anchors, rationale, source IDs, and audit status are preserved in
`config/scenario_assumptions.csv`.

## Scenario stories

The **base case** assumes FY2027 remains pressured as Nike Direct and digital trends,
Converse weakness, Greater China conditions, and cautious wholesale purchasing
offset improving performance-product and sport-category momentum. FY2028 represents
stabilization: marketplace inventory and promotional activity become less disruptive,
while full-price realization and distribution improve gradually. FY2029-FY2031
assume broader product momentum, demand-creation investment, supply-chain efficiency,
and operating-overhead leverage support a measured recovery. Tariffs, foreign
exchange, logistics, consumer demand, and traffic remain constraints rather than
disappearing. Capex rises from 1.5% to 1.7% of revenue and ONWC normalizes toward
10.0%. These are conditions embedded in the scenario, not predicted outcomes.

The **bull case** assumes FY2027 stabilization arrives sooner because performance
products and sport categories gain traction, full-price sales improve, and Nike
Direct, digital, wholesale distribution, Greater China, and Converse perform better
than in base. FY2028 builds on cleaner marketplace inventory and less promotional
pressure. FY2029-FY2031 assume demand creation and product investment translate into
broader revenue growth, supply-chain execution improves, and SG&A gains leverage even
as absolute spending rises. Higher capex supports the stronger growth path, and ONWC
efficiency improves most. Favorable execution must outweigh tariff, currency,
logistics, demand, and traffic risks; it is not presumed from the official sources.

The **bear case** assumes FY2027 pressure persists across Nike Direct and digital,
wholesale buyers keep purchases tight, Greater China and Converse remain weak, and
promotional activity limits full-price realization. FY2028 represents continued
deterioration rather than stabilization, with demand-creation spending and operating
overhead remaining relatively rigid. FY2029-FY2031 allow only a slow recovery as
product initiatives and marketplace inventory actions take longer to support sales
and margins. Tariff, foreign-exchange, logistics, consumer-demand, and traffic risks
remain more pronounced. Capex stays positive at a maintenance-oriented level and
ONWC remains elevated, although its FY2027 balance still declines from FY2026.

## FY2031 endpoints

The FY2031 endpoints are operating states used by the separately documented Phase 5
terminal bridge. They are not terminal-value assumptions. Phase 4 itself does not
specify perpetual growth, return on capital, discount rates, or valuation.

## Evidence boundary

The FY2026 10-K and official results release establish the reported starting point.
Official company commentary informs near-term risks and recovery considerations but
does not supply these full five-year scenario paths. Each numeric path is therefore
classified as an analyst assumption, with its audit status recorded separately from
the company sources.
