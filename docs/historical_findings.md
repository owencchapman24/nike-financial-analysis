# Historical Findings

## Scope

This independent portfolio analysis evaluates Nike's FY2022-FY2026 historical
financial performance using reproducible data derived from official SEC filings. It
is historical analysis, not an investment recommendation.

The observations below describe changes in the validated data. They do not establish
why the changes occurred. Causal explanations require separate support from specific
filing disclosures.

## Key historical findings

### 1. Revenue peaked in FY2024 and finished close to its FY2022 level

Revenue increased from $46,710 million in FY2022 to $51,217 million in FY2023 and
reached $51,362 million in FY2024. From that peak, revenue declined $4,964 million,
or 9.7%, to $46,398 million in FY2026. That left FY2026 revenue $312 million, or 0.7%,
below FY2022. The corresponding five-observation CAGR was -0.2% across four growth
intervals.

### 2. Profitability ended below FY2022 across all three margin measures

Gross margin decreased from 46.0% in FY2022 to 42.9% in FY2026, down 3.1 percentage
points. Derived operating margin decreased from 14.3% to 8.2%, down 6.1 points, and
net margin decreased from 12.9% to 6.7%, down 6.2 points. Over the same period, total
SG&A increased from 31.7% to 34.7% of revenue, up 3.0 points. These trends occurred
together, but this analysis does not attribute the full margin change to SG&A.

Derived operating income is calculated as gross profit less total selling and
administrative expense. Nike does not report this consolidated operating-income
subtotal, and the project measure is not segment EBIT.

### 3. Cash generation peaked in FY2024 and was lower by FY2026

Operating cash flow increased from $5,188 million in FY2022 to $7,429 million in
FY2024, then decreased to $2,868 million in FY2026. Free cash flow declined $4,433
million, or 67.0%, from its $6,617 million FY2024 peak to $2,184 million in FY2026.
Free-cash-flow margin moved from 9.5% in FY2022 to 12.9% in FY2024 and 4.7% in
FY2026.

Cash conversion is operating cash flow divided by net income. It was 0.86x in FY2022,
peaked at 1.30x in FY2024, and was 0.92x in FY2026, below the 1.0x reference. Capital
expenditures are shown as positive investment amounts when calculating free cash
flow.

### 4. Liquidity measures decreased while FY2026 receivables increased

Cash plus short-term investments decreased from $12,997 million in FY2022 to
$9,027 million in FY2026. The current ratio moved from 2.63x to 1.96x, and broad net
working capital decreased from $17,483 million to $12,056 million.

Accounts receivable increased 25.7% in FY2026, compared with 0.2% revenue growth.
The receivables-days proxy increased from 36.0 days in FY2025 to 41.9 days in FY2026.
This measure uses average receivables and total revenue, not separately disclosed
credit sales, so it is not DSO. Inventory was approximately $7,500 million in both
FY2025 and FY2026, and inventory days were approximately 103.3 days in both years.
FY2022 efficiency days are not applicable because FY2021 opening balances are not in
the committed dataset.

### 5. Debt decreased, but the net-cash buffer also narrowed

Interest-bearing debt decreased from $9,430 million in FY2022 to $7,942 million in
FY2026. This definition includes notes payable or short-term borrowings, current
maturities of long-term debt, and noncurrent long-term debt. Operating lease
liabilities are separate and totaled $3,091 million in FY2026.

Signed net debt equals interest-bearing debt less cash and short-term investments.
It was negative in every year, indicating net cash under this convention, but the
net-cash buffer narrowed by $2,482 million, from $3,567 million in FY2022 to $1,085
million in FY2026. The underlying signed values are -$3,567 million and -$1,085
million; the FY2026 result is described as net cash, not negative debt.

## Interpretation limits

The analysis contains five annual observations and cannot show quarterly seasonality
or establish a durable trend by itself. The derived operating subtotal, D&A tag
transition, lease-exclusion convention, and receivables proxy all affect how the
results should be interpreted. See [limitations.md](limitations.md) and
[methodology.md](methodology.md) for the full definitions.
